How approval works

The switch decides who approves. These rules always win: an application they flag waits for you even with auto-approve on.

  1. One application per role. The submitter refuses a second application to the same company and role (or the same requisition ID). Duplicates are blocked, not queued.
  2. Company limits. If a company caps how many roles you can apply to, every application there needs your approval so you can choose.
  3. Manual-only sites. CAPTCHAs, Workday account sign-ups, and LinkedIn Easy Apply (automation breaks LinkedIn's terms) go to Needs you with a prepared pack and the link.
  4. One automatic attempt. If a submission errors, the agent stops after that attempt and lists it under Needs you. It never retries on its own, so nothing is sent twice.
  5. Deal-breakers. Fully onsite roles, a posted top salary under $300K, or below VP level are never auto-approved.
  6. Low or unclear fit. Fit under 75, or a posting with open questions (office days, location), waits for you.
  7. Never without a record. Every submission saves a confirmation screenshot and updates the tracker; nothing is sent from this page itself.
